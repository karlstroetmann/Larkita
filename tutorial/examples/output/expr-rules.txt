Every rule is followed by the abstract syntax tree that a reduction produces.
"*name" stands for the list of children collected by the auxiliary rule "name".
"text" stands for the text of a named token.

  (0)   $root_stmnt → stmnt
  (1)   stmnt → IDENTIFIER ":=" expr ";"  ⇒ ((":=", ";"), ("IDENTIFIER", text), ast(expr))
  (2)   stmnt → expr ";"                  ⇒ (";", ast(expr))
  (3)   expr → expr "+" product           ⇒ ("+", ast(expr), ast(product))
  (4)   expr → expr "-" product           ⇒ ("-", ast(expr), ast(product))
  (5)   expr → product                    ⇒ ast(product)
  (6)   product → product "*" factor      ⇒ ("*", ast(product), ast(factor))
  (7)   product → product "/" factor      ⇒ ("/", ast(product), ast(factor))
  (8)   product → factor                  ⇒ ast(factor)
  (9)   factor → "(" expr ")"             ⇒ ast(expr)
  (10)  factor → NUMBER                   ⇒ ("NUMBER", text)
  (11)  factor → IDENTIFIER               ⇒ ("IDENTIFIER", text)
