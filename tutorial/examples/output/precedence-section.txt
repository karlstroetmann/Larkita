The precedence increases from top to bottom.

  %nonassoc    "<" "=="
  %left        "+" "-"
  %left        "*" "/"
  %precedence  UMINUS
  %right       "^"

A rule has the precedence of the symbol after %prec or, without %prec, of its
last terminal.  These rules have a precedence:

  (1)  expr → expr "<" expr   "<"
  (2)  expr → expr "==" expr  "=="
  (3)  expr → expr "+" expr   "+"
  (4)  expr → expr "-" expr   "-"
  (5)  expr → expr "*" expr   "*"
  (6)  expr → expr "/" expr   "/"
  (7)  expr → expr "^" expr   "^"
  (8)  expr → "-" expr        UMINUS
